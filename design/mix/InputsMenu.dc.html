Screen
Each small view shows what that input sees and names it. Click one and its list opens upward, over the picture's lower edge, and closes when you choose. The four input buttons leave the lower left. Keys 1 to 4 and MIDI still switch A in one press; Shift + 1 to 4 switch B. With no Mix B in the preset, A's view stays, and the bar and B are dimmed.
