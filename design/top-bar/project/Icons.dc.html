MIDI map
M1The five-pin socket. Says MIDI and nothing else. Recommended.
M2Piano keys, as in Ableton. Familiar, but Setup already has a panel called Keys (the computer's), and nothing here is played on a keyboard.
M3A knob. Says "a physical control", which is what you map; less plainly MIDI.
M4MIDIThe word alone, without "map". The shortest thing that needs no learning.
Any of these fills in, as the other buttons do, while map mode is on.
Record
R1Rec0:42
A ring and the word; recording, the ring fills, the button blinks and shows the time. In the interface's one colour. Recommended.
R2
The ring alone. Smallest, but nowhere to show how long it has been recording, or that the file is growing.
R3Rec
A true red dot, whatever the palette. It breaks the one-colour rule, and in the red palette it is not red against anything.
Colour and dimmer
P1A half-filled disc: the usual sign for appearance, and it hints at the dimmer too. It cannot be taken for a record light. Recommended.
P2Three small swatches, one filled: "choose one of several".
P3The dot as it is today. Fine by itself; beside a record ring it reads as a second light.
